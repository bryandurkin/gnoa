# GNOA Website: Photo and Logo Checklist

Greater Naples Officials Association

The draft site uses sample photos for now. Send us the real ones below and we swap them in. Please name each file exactly as shown (or just tell us which photo goes where and we will name them).

**How to send photos**
- Original files straight from the camera or phone (JPG or PNG). No screenshots, no filters, no text or logos on the photo.
- Bigger is better. Anything at or above the listed size works; we crop and resize.
- Horizontal (landscape) photos unless the row says portrait.
- Only photos GNOA has the right to use, of people who are OK being on the website.
- For each photo, a quick note on who or what is in it helps us write the captions and descriptions search engines read.
