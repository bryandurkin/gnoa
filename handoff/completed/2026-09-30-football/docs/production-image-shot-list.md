# Production Image Shot List

The PNGs in `reference-images/` are **visual crops from the approved AI mockup**, not original source photography. Use them for composition, crop, mood, and subject placement. Replace them with final GNOA-owned or properly licensed images when available.

## Recommended final photo assets

1. **hero-football-official** — 16:9 or 21:9, 2400px+ wide. Referee foreground, stadium/palm/sunset atmosphere, negative space left for copy.
2. **mentor-new-official** — 3:2, 1600px+. Experienced official talking to newer official on field; warm and approachable.
3. **rules-positioning-mechanics** — 5 supporting crops, approx. 4:3. Rulebook/game card; official positioning; signal/mechanics; crew conference; official/coach communication.
4. **first-game-night** — 16:9, 2200px+. Official from behind under Friday-night lights with field action soft in background.
5. **experienced-crew** — 3:2, 1600px+. Multi-person football crew huddle/pregame meeting.
6. **new-official-training** — 4:3, 1200px+. Mechanics/rules/film session with officials visible.
7. **friday-night-football** — 4:3, 1200px+. Live high-school game; official prominent.
8. **postseason-assignment** — 4:3, 1200px+. Crew walking/working playoff game.
9. **championship-crew** — 4:3, 1200px+. Crew at state-level venue or championship setting.
10. **official-equipment-flatlay** — 4:3, 1200px+. Black cap, whistle, flags, down indicator, cards/timing tools; no commercial-brand emphasis.
11. **final-cta-field-view** — 16:9 or 21:9, 2200px+. Official looking over field/crowd under lights; strong emotional close.

## Photo direction
- Officials are always the primary subject.
- Players are contextual background only.
- Favor real GNOA officials whenever possible.
- Mix ages and experience levels naturally.
- Avoid staged handshake/corporate stock imagery.
- Southwest Florida atmosphere can be conveyed with palm silhouettes, warm light, local stadiums, and subtropical surroundings without becoming touristy.
