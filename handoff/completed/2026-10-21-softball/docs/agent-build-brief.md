# Agent Build Brief — GNOA Softball Recruiting Page

## Source of truth
1. `reference-images/00-full-page-mockup.png` = visual authority.
2. `docs/page-content.md` = content/presentation direction.
3. The **latest simplified mockup** overrides the earlier busy softball concept.

## Design goal
Build a cleaner, easier-to-read softball page in the established GNOA family. It should feel modern, athletic, credible, welcoming, and community-driven — without looking crowded or templated.

## What changed from the earlier softball mockup
- More white space.
- Fewer bordered cards.
- Shorter paragraphs.
- Bigger headings and clearer hierarchy.
- More image-led sections.
- Larger icon treatments.
- No visible numbered steps.
- Avoid dense checklists where a short list will do.
- Give each section one clear job.

## Layout
### Header
Shared GNOA header. Keep navigation compact and the recruiting CTA obvious.

### Hero
Two-column desktop hero: dark navy copy panel left, authentic softball umpire action photo right. Maintain strong contrast and generous padding.

### Benefits
Four simple benefit items with large circular icons. These should feel open, not like heavy cards.

### Training
Use a large image block paired with a concise learning list and one small support callout. Avoid making this a wall of copy.

### Getting started
Five milestones shown horizontally on desktop. Use icons and arrows or spacing — **no 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 numbers**. Stack cleanly on mobile.

### Good umpire skills
Five concise skill items. Prefer icon + short copy. Pair the section with a strong softball umpire action photo.

### Lower-page continuation
For experienced officials, development, equipment, FAQs, and final CTA, continue the same low-density system. Use alternating image/text bands, open typography, and one clear CTA per major section.

## Photography
- Officials are always the main visual subject.
- Softball umpires may wear appropriate softball/baseball-style caps where authentic to the sport.
- Use real GNOA photography whenever possible.
- Avoid stock-photo collage styling.
- Avoid overly staged or corporate poses.

## Responsive behavior
- Hero stacks text above image on mobile.
- Benefits become 2x2, then 1-column.
- Training image and text stack vertically.
- Getting-started milestones become a vertical sequence without numbered labels.
- Skills can become 2 columns, then 1 column.

## Accessibility
- WCAG AA contrast.
- Visible focus states.
- Semantic heading order.
- Useful alt text for photos.
- Decorative SVG icons `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Do not bake page copy into image files.

## Asset note
The photo crops in this package are extracted from the AI-generated mockup. They are **reference/prototype assets only**, not original source photos. Replace them with GNOA-owned or properly licensed photography for launch.
