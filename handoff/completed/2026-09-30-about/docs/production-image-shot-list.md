# Production Image Shot List — Concise About Page

The mockup uses generated composite photography. For production, replace with GNOA-owned or properly licensed images that preserve the same compositions.

## 1. Hero — officials community
**Use:** Hero background/right-side image  
**Recommended source size:** 2200 × 1000 px or larger  
**Composition:** 4–6 officials in a relaxed pregame/training conversation; mix of sports if possible; faces visible; one or two female officials if authentic to available photography; palm trees / warm Southwest Florida light helpful.  
**Avoid:** player-dominant scenes, staged corporate poses, unrelated youth-team photos.

## 2. Seven sport tiles
**Use:** Football, Basketball, Baseball, Softball, Soccer, Volleyball, Wrestling  
**Recommended source size per tile:** minimum 900 × 1100 px so the build can crop portrait/4:5.  
**Composition:** one identifiable official or officiating action per sport. Officials should be visually dominant.

## 3. More Than the Game — community photo
**Use:** right-side image beside skill cards  
**Recommended source size:** 1400 × 1200 px  
**Composition:** small group of officials talking, laughing, mentoring, or huddling; candid rather than posed.

## 4. Development progression — four images
**Recommended source size:** minimum 1000 × 700 px each  
- New Official: new/younger official receiving guidance.
- Crew Member: two or more officials preparing or talking together.
- Varsity Assignment: official working a live game under lights.
- Postseason Opportunity: experienced official/crew in a more significant assignment environment.

## 5. Final CTA — field perspective
**Use:** closing banner  
**Recommended source size:** 2200 × 800 px or larger  
**Composition:** official viewed from behind/three-quarter angle looking toward a field or court; warm sunset/night lights; negative space on the left for copy.

## Image treatment
- Natural color first; light grade toward warm Florida light is fine.
- Avoid aggressive HDR, heavy blue overlays baked into source images, or obvious stock-photo posing.
- Use CSS overlay gradients for text readability rather than permanently darkening original images.
