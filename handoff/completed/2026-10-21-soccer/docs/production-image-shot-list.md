## Photography rules
- No baseball/softball-style caps on soccer referees unless an actual approved local situation calls for it.
- Prioritize officials over players.
- Favor real GNOA people and Southwest Florida fields.
- Avoid generic corporate/staged imagery.
- Secure appropriate usage rights/releases.
