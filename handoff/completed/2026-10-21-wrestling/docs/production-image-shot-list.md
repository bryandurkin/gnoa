## Photography rules
- Officials, not wrestlers, should remain the primary visual subject whenever possible.
- Show authentic mat-level positioning and movement.
- Use proper wrestling officiating apparel and red/green wristbands when appropriate.
- Avoid posed corporate-style imagery.
- Prioritize actual GNOA officials and Southwest Florida venues when available.
- Secure appropriate usage rights/releases.
