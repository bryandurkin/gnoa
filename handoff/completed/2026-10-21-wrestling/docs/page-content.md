## What Makes a Good Wrestling Official?

### Positioning
Officials need to move constantly to maintain the best possible view of the action.

The right angle helps you recognize control, scoring, and safety situations.

### Rules Knowledge
Wrestling has detailed rules involving scoring, control, penalties, boundaries, and match situations.

Training helps you learn how to apply them in real time.

### Judgment
Not every situation is obvious.

Good officials learn to recognize what happened, determine control, and make clear decisions without guessing.

### Communication
Signals, voice, body language, and communication with coaches, wrestlers, and the scorer’s table all matter.

### Composure
Matches can become intense.

Good officials stay calm, professional, and focused on the action in front of them.

## Wrestling Officiating FAQs

### Do I need previous officiating experience?
No. GNOA trains and develops new wrestling officials.

### Do I need to have wrestled?
No. Wrestling experience can help with familiarity, but officiating is its own skill and can be learned.

### Will I start with varsity matches?
New officials develop over time. Assignments should match your training, experience, and readiness.

### Is wrestling officiating physically demanding?
Officials need to move constantly around the mat, change levels, and maintain good positioning throughout the match.

### Is safety part of the official’s job?
Yes. Officials are responsible for recognizing potentially dangerous situations and protecting the competitors while allowing legal action to continue.

### Should I buy equipment now?
Talk with GNOA first. We can help you understand current requirements and whether used or hand-me-down equipment may be available.
