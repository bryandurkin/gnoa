# Production Image Shot List — GNOA Volleyball Recruiting Page

Replace mockup photo references with GNOA-owned or properly licensed images whenever possible.

| Priority | Use | Recommended composition | Suggested size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | Volleyball official on stand or near net making a clear signal; gym context; negative space for left-side copy | 2000×1200+ |
| 2 | Training | Experienced official instructing or observing a newer official, or close court action that emphasizes net/positioning | 1600×1100+ |
| 3 | Skills | Details showing signals, court positioning, scorer-table communication, and match focus | 1000×800+ each |
| 4 | Experienced official | First/second referee or officiating crew talking pre-match or between sets | 1400×1000+ |
| 5 | Development | Clinic/training, local match, postseason/advanced assignment, mentoring | 1000×700+ each |
| 6 | Equipment | Approved shirt, whistle/lanyard, court shoes, watch/match materials | 1200×900+ |
| 7 | Final CTA | Volleyball on court or official looking over court; strong text-safe area | 2000×900+ |

## Photography rules
- Officials should remain the main subject whenever possible.
- Prioritize real GNOA people and recognizable Southwest Florida venues if available.
- Avoid generic corporate imagery and player-first stock photography.
- Keep images credible, documentary, and contemporary.
- Secure appropriate usage rights/releases.
